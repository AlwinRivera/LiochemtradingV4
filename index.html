What We Offer

Our Cleaning &
Hygiene Solutions

From kitchen warewashing to full-facility sanitation programs, our specialized product lines and on-site services deliver measurable hygiene outcomes for every industry.

Warewashing Chemicals

High-performance detergents and rinse aids for commercial dishwashers, glasswashers, and pot-washing systems in restaurants and hotels.

Housekeeping Chemicals

Multi-surface cleaners, degreasers, and disinfectants formulated for hotels, offices, hospitals, and institutional facilities.

Clean-In-Place (CIP)

Alkaline and acid CIP cleaners for dairy, beverage, and food processing plants — designed to clean pipelines and vessels without disassembly.

Foam Cleaning

Thick-foam detergents that cling to vertical surfaces for extended contact time — ideal for meat processing, bakeries, and food production.

Conveyor Lubrication

Wet and dry lubricants that reduce friction, extend conveyor belt life, and maintain food-safe compliance in bottling and packaging lines.

Total Hygiene Management

End-to-end sanitation programs including audits, training, documentation, and chemical supply — all under one partner.

Bottle Washing

Caustic and mild bottle-wash cleaners for glass and PET bottle reprocessing in beverage and water refilling industries.

Personal Hygiene

Hand soaps, sanitizers, and hygiene dispensing systems for workplaces, food facilities, and public-facing businesses.

Pest Control

Chemical and program-based pest management solutions to maintain hygienic, pest-free environments in compliance with food safety regulations.
